<p>Dear Alumni,</p>
<p>Teachers’ Day is a special time to reconnect with the teachers who have
been part of your journey, and we are always happy to have our alumni back
at Bukit Timah Primary School.</p>
<p>If you would like to visit your former teachers on Thursday, 3 September
2026, please take note of the following arrangements to help make the visit
a pleasant one for everyone</p>
<p>1. Arrange your visit with your teachers:</p>
<p>Please check if your teachers are available on the day and make arrangements
directly with them. Upon arrival, meet your teachers at the school gate.</p>
<p>2. Visiting hours: 11 a.m. to 12.30 p.m.</p>
<p>Our students will be dismissed before 11.00 a.m. If you arrive earlier,
please wait without blocking the school entrances or interfering with the
dismissal process. We also encourage you to keep your visit brief, so that
our teachers can have some time to rest after the morning celebrations.</p>
<p>3. Visiting areas:</p>
<p>For the safety and convenience of everyone, please keep your visit to
the ground-floor areas of the school only.</p>
<p>Thank you for your understanding and cooperation in helping us keep the
school safe and welcoming for everyone. We look forward to seeing you back
at BTPS and hope you enjoy catching up with your teachers.</p>
<p>Happy Teachers’ Day!</p>
<p>Bukit Timah Primary School</p>
